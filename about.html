As a GIS specialist, I have an extensive background in field data collection, drone mapping, and remote sensing, which informs my work in spatial expertise. I focus on producing accurate and structured geospatial outputs to develop digital solutions that simplify complex spatial problems.

This WebGIS-based spatial suitability analysis platform for retail expansion planning in Tangerang Selatan as part of the MAPID WebGIS Development Bootcamp. The web mapping project I built from scratch. The platform applies weighted overlay methodology across multiple criteria including population density, road accessibility, competitor proximity, and POI concentration to classify and visualize location suitability scores interactively on the web.

South Tangerang (Tangsel) is one of the fastest-growing urban areas in the Jakarta metropolitan region, yet retail growth has been spatially uneven. High-density commercial zones like BSD City and Alam Sutera concentrate the majority of retail activity, while residential areas in districts such as Ciater and Sawah Baru remain significantly underserved — despite continuous population growth. Retail entrepreneurs entering this market typically rely on intuition or anecdotal knowledge when choosing a location. There is no accessible, data-driven tool that maps competitor density, transit accessibility, and road networks in a single platform. Retailscope was built to fill that gap: a WebGIS platform that enables users to identify strategic retail locations in South Tangerang through spatial suitability analysis.
